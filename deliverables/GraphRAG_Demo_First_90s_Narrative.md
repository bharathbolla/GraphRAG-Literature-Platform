# GraphRAG Literature Platform

90-second narrative: project introduction, tool walkthrough, pharma use cases, closing.

## 00:00–00:05 | Project introduction

**00:00–00:05**
Graph RAG helps pharma teams find, connect, and compare research evidence.

## 00:05–01:00 | Using the tool

**00:05–00:11**
Start by searching PubMed. Click Ingest to add matching papers.

**00:11–00:18**
Papers increase from three to seven. Section-aware text chunks grow from ten to thirty-two.

**00:18–00:24**
Extract entities and PICO fields. Normalize synonyms to avoid duplicate concept nodes.

**00:24–00:31**
Entities grow from eighteen to thirty-two, edges from thirty-four to eighty-eight, and communities from five to eight.

**00:31–00:36**
The graph view expands from twenty-one to thirty-nine nodes, connecting studies and concepts.

**00:36–00:44**
Hybrid Graph RAG follows study connections, then ranks passages. This supports cross-paper questions beyond similarity search alone.

**00:44–00:49**
Browse normalized concepts in NER, or compare studies in PICO.

**00:49–00:55**
Compare populations, interventions, comparators, and outcomes, then export the evidence.

**00:55–01:00**
Check the evidence table and trace citations to source sentences.

## 01:00–01:25 | Potential uses across pharma

**01:00–01:08**
R and D can investigate targets. Clinical development can compare trials, and medical affairs can prepare cited answers.

**01:08–01:16**
Market access teams can compare outcomes. Safety teams can investigate published findings across studies.

**01:16–01:25**
Regulatory reviewers can trace claims to source passages, helping teams check evidence and identify gaps for further review.

## 01:25–01:30 | Closing

**01:25–01:30**
One shared evidence base, with findings linked to source passages.
